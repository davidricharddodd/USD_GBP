Purpose: A high-stakes, precision FX monitoring terminal designed for individuals and businesses executing large currency transfers.
Tone: Executive Fintech / Modern Terminal. Dark, sharp, and highly utilitarian with neon data accents.
Inspiration: Bloomberg Terminal, high-end crypto exchanges, Linear.

Key Principles:
- Dark Mode Only: Deep midnight backgrounds reduce eye strain during continuous monitoring and elevate the perceived value of the data.
- Absolute Precision: 0px border radius across all UI elements. Sharp corners communicate accuracy, seriousness, and lack of compromise.
- Typographic Hierarchy: 'Space Grotesk' for striking, geometric headers. 'JetBrains Mono' is heavily utilized for all financial figures, rates, and timestamps to ensure tabular alignment and technical precision.
- High-Contrast Data: Neon cyan and amber act as primary data and alert colors, piercing through the dark background to immediately draw attention to rate drops and target thresholds.
- Depth through Borders: Instead of soft drop shadows, depth is established through subtle, crisp 1px borders and slight background lightness variations.
